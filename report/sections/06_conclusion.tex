Although the evaluation results are likely biased towards better studied diseases

and show only moderate prediction success, it is still surprising that a pathway similarity metric, based on very simplified assumptions,
combined with simple chemical and molecular descriptors can be predictive of a success.

Future directions for ameliorating our approach would be:
\begin{itemize}
    \item consider non-protein targets and biopolymer drugs
    \item include directionality of the association with target
    \item extend the scope of studied diseases
    \item integrate labels from other sources
    \item continue exploring Graph Neural Networks to model molecular interaction network
    \item try semisupervised Positive Unlabeled models
\end{itemize}
