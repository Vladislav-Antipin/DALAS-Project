\section{Conclusion}

Although the evaluation results are likely biased toward better-studied diseases and 
demonstrate only moderate predictive performance, 
it is notable that a pathway similarity metric, based on simplified assumptions, combined with basic 
chemical and molecular descriptors can still provide meaningful predictions.

Future directions for ameliorating our approach would be:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item considering non-protein targets and biopolymer drugs,
    \item incorporating the directionality of associations with targets,
    \item expanding the scope of studied diseases,
    \item integrating labels from additional data sources,
    \item further exploring Graph Neural Networks to model molecular interaction networks, and
    \item going further in interpretation of pathway embeddings and molecular fingerprints by
     relating them back to the original interpretable pathways and chemical structures.
\end{itemize}
